What’s a good time?

Most people land between 200 and 300 ms. Your result varies with your device, screen and input method, so compare your own attempts to each other more than to anyone else’s.

TimeRating
Under 150 msExceptional, or a very lucky guess
150–200 msExcellent
200–250 msGood
250–350 msAverage
Over 350 msSlow. Try again when you’re rested.

Notes

Five lights come on, one second apart. After the fifth, there is a random wait of one to four seconds and then they all go out. Press as soon as they do.

Pressing before they go out is a jump start and is not recorded. Results are kept in this browser only.
